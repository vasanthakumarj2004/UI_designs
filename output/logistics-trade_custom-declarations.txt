From document intake to syncing entry data with your customs software — automatically, so your team reviews clean data instead of retyping it.
Collect customs documents → Sync to customs system

## Customs entry prep is still a costly manual bottleneck
Your team is retyping the same data from documents into your customs software — every single entry.

## From Document intake to customs entry sync — in 4 steps

### Collect
Gathers key customs documents — commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading — from email, shared drives, and APIs.

### Extract
AI extracts and structures the data needed for customs entries, including line‑level detail from commercial invoices.

### Validate
Matches related documents to the correct entry, checks for missing or inconsistent data, and alerts users before issues cause clearance delays.

### Sync
Automatically creates or updates customs entry records in your system for your team to review — clean data, ready to file.

## Precision automation built for customs teams

  • Line-level extraction — From commercial invoices for accurate entry data — every item, every charge.
