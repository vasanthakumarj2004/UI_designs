# Welcome to Docsynecx
Docsynecx is an AI-powered document processing platform that automates invoice extraction, validation, and accounting integration.
It helps businesses process invoices faster and reduces manual data entry by automatically extracting invoice information and syncing it with accounting systems such as Tally .

## Key Features
Docsynecx provides powerful automation features for invoice processing:
  • AI-Based Invoice Extraction – Automatically extract data from invoices.
  • Item-Level and Accounting-Level Processing – Support for item invoices and ledger-based invoices.
  • Automatic Validation – Detect mismatches in tax, totals, or round-off values.
  • Middleware Integration – Seamless connection with accounting systems like Tally.
  • Automated Voucher Creation – Convert invoices directly into accounting vouchers.

## Typical Workflow
The standard invoice processing workflow in Docsynecx is:
  • Upload Invoice
  • Automatic Data Extraction
  • Review & Validate Invoice
  • Detect Mismatches
  • Save or Sync to Tally
  • Store Documents in Cloud Archive
