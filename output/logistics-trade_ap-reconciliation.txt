## AP reconciliation is eating your finance team's time
Your team is spending hours each week on invoice-to-TMS matching that should take seconds.

## Invoice intake to reconciled TMS data — in 4 steps

### Collect
Gathers invoices from all sources — email, shared drives, and APIs — automatically, as they arrive.

### Extract
AI extracts and structures key invoice data — charges, vendors, dates, and totals — with precision.

### Map & Validate
Matches invoice line items to the correct charge codes and validates each charge against what's expected in your TMS.

### Sync
Pushes structured, validated invoice data directly into your TMS — clean records, ready for payment approval.
