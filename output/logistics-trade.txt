# AI Logistics Automation for Freight Forwarders
Automate freight forwarding operations with AI-powered document processing for shipment creation, customs documentation, AP reconciliation, and Cargowise integration.

## Documents In. Shipments Out. Less Busywork.
Start with one workflow or automate them all. Each module handles the full document-to-data pipeline independently.

### Create & update shipments instantly
Automate shipment creation and updates from incoming logistics documents. Extract and validate critical shipment data, apply business rules, and synchronize accurate records directly with your TMS.

### Pre-fill customs forms automatically
Streamline customs data entry by converting source documents into structured declaration data. Validate line-level details, enforce compliance rules, and transfer verified entries directly to your customs system.

### Reconcile AP invoices in real time
Automate AP reconciliation by extracting invoice charges, mapping them to TMS codes, and validating them against expected costs. Surface discrepancies early and keep financial records aligned with shipment activity.
