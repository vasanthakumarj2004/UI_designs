## From Document intake to shipment creation — in 4 steps

### Collect
Brings shipping documents together from email, shared drives, APIs, and other business systems—all in one place.

### Extract
AI extracts and structures key shipment data fields with precision — container numbers, parties, dates, weights, and more.

### Validate
Matches related documents to the correct shipment, checks for missing or inconsistent data, and alerts users before issues cause delays.

### Sync
Creates and updates shipment records in your TMS with validated data — automatically and accurately, around the clock.
