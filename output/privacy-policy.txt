When registering or using Docsynecx, we may voluntarily collect Personal Data for service performance. This may include:

When using Docsynecx, users typically upload documents (PDFs, scans, etc.) via the app, email, API, or third-party integrations. These documents (Imported Documents) are processed to extract structured information (Parsed Content).

## 2. Usage of Data
We collect data to improve user experience and service functionality. Data is retained as necessary unless the user requests deletion via privacy@docsynecx.com . Upon such request, data will be deleted within 30 days with confirmation.
We do not sell Personal Data, Imported Documents, or Parsed Content. Documents may be retained for AI training and product development and will be deleted post-use. This processing is based on legitimate interest under applicable data protection laws.
Sensitive data (e.g., racial, political, religious, health-related) must not be uploaded or processed through Docsynecx.
