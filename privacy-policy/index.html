Last updated: October 2026 · SynecxAI Labs Private Limited

1. Information We Collect

Docsynecx collects information necessary to provide our intelligent document processing services, including account credentials, business billing information, and document content submitted for extraction.

2. How We Use Document Data

Documents uploaded to Docsynecx are processed exclusively for data extraction, validation, and synchronization to your designated ERP or TMS systems. We do not sell or share customer document data with third parties.

3. Data Protection & Security

All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption and encrypted at rest. We implement strict role-based access control and isolation between customer workspaces.
