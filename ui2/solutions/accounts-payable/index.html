Capabilities

What Docsynecx does for Accounts Payable

Tailored automation features built for how your team actually works.

Multi-Channel Invoice Intake

Collect invoices from dedicated email inboxes, vendor portals, WhatsApp, and API submissions into one unified queue.

AI Data Extraction

Extract vendor name, GSTIN, invoice number, date, line items, tax amounts, and totals from any document format.

GSTIN & Arithmetic Validation

Automatically verify tax registration numbers, check arithmetic totals, and detect duplicate invoice submissions.

Multi-Level Approval Routing

Route invoices to the right approver based on amount thresholds, cost centres, and vendor categories — with escalation rules.

ERP & Accounting Sync

Post approved vouchers directly into Tally, Zoho Books, SAP, and custom ERPs without any manual intervention.

Exception Dashboard

Track every invoice from intake to posting with a real-time dashboard showing status, exceptions, and aging invoices.

How it works

From documents to done, in four steps.

Step 01

Capture

Collect documents from email, portals, APIs and uploads automatically into a single queue.

Step 02

Extract

AI reads every document and outputs structured, field-level data ready for validation.

Step 03

Validate & Route

Business rules check every field and send exceptions to the right reviewer automatically.

Step 04

Sync

Approved data posts directly into your ERP, TMS, or accounting system — zero rekeying.
